Case study · Project 01

Finance Management Software for Schools, Colleges, Hospitals & Gyms

A complete finance and operations platform that replaces spreadsheets and paper registers: income, expenses and profit in real time, plus members, staff, fees, payments, inventory and reports in one place.

  • Full-Stack
  • Analytics Dashboard
  • Financial Reporting
  • Admin Roles

The problem

Schools, clinics and gyms run on recurring payments, yet most still track them in registers and spreadsheets. Fees go uncollected because nobody can see who owes what, expenses are recorded late or not at all, and the owner only learns whether the month was profitable long after it ends.

The solution

One web application where every rupee in and out is recorded once and reported everywhere. The dashboard answers the questions an owner asks every day — how much came in, how much went out, what is left, and who still has to pay — for any period, in a single click.

Modules

Dashboard

Income, expenses, profit and member KPIs with Today, 7 days, 1/3/6 months, This year, All time or a custom date range.

Members

Students, patients or gym members with status tracking (active and inactive).

Staff & trainers

Teachers, doctors or trainers linked to the people they serve.

Monthly fees

Generate fees in bulk and track every open bill and the total outstanding.

Additional charges

One-off items such as admission, lab, exam or locker charges.

Payments

Record payments in seconds and keep a full history per member.

Expenses

Rent, salaries, utilities and supplies, categorised for reporting.

Inventory

Stock of products, equipment and consumables.

Reports

Income-versus-expenses cash flow and financial reports for any period.

Designed for speed of use

The most common tasks — Add Member, Record Payment, Generate Fees and Add Expense — sit in a Quick Actions bar on the dashboard, so front-desk staff can do their daily work without hunting through menus. Access is managed through administrator roles.

One platform, four industries

  • Schools & colleges: student fee generation, defaulter tracking and staff salaries.
  • Hospitals & clinics: patient charges, payments and medical supply inventory.
  • Gyms & fitness studios: monthly memberships, trainers and supplement stock.
  • Any membership business: anywhere recurring billing and expense control matter.

Frequently asked questions

Which organisations can use this finance management software?

It is built for any organisation that bills members or clients on a recurring basis: schools and colleges (students and fees), hospitals and clinics (patients and charges), and gyms and fitness studios (members and monthly fees).

What does the dashboard show?

Income, expenses, profit and member counts for any date range — today, 7 days, 1 month, 3 or 6 months, this year, all time or a custom range — plus an income-versus-expenses chart and the status of open fee bills.
