FitX — 3D Gym Website with Admin Panel & Chatbot

A premium, scroll-driven 3D website for FitX Fitness Studio in Sahiwal, Punjab — backed by a custom admin panel the staff use to publish content, and a chatbot that answers visitors around the clock.

  • Three.js · WebGL
  • Admin Panel / CMS
  • Chatbot
  • Full-Stack

The goal

FitX positions itself as the most premium training studio in Sahiwal, offering one-on-one coaching built around each client’s body, schedule and goals. The website had to communicate that premium feel instantly — and keep working for the business after launch without a developer on call.

What I built

1. An immersive 3D experience

The site opens on a real-time WebGL scene: concentric glowing rings, drifting particles and a 3D dumbbell, rendered with Three.js. Visitors “scroll to enter”, and the camera moves through the space as they explore the studio’s offer, timings (Saturday–Thursday, Friday closed) and location.

2. An admin panel for the gym team

A custom admin panel lets staff publish blog posts, upload photos and update other site data on their own. Fresh content keeps the site useful for members and gives search engines new pages to index.

3. A chatbot for instant answers

A built-in chatbot answers the questions prospective members ask most — memberships, timings and training — at any hour, turning late-night visitors into enquiries instead of lost leads.

4. Conversion first

Clear Book and WhatsApp calls to action are always one tap away, on desktop and mobile.

Technology

  • Three.js / WebGL for the real-time 3D scene and scroll-driven camera.
  • Admin panel / CMS for blogs, photos and site data.
  • Chatbot integration for visitor questions.
  • Responsive layout tuned for mobile, where most gym visitors browse.

Frequently asked questions

What makes the FitX website different from a normal gym website?

Instead of a static template, visitors scroll through a real-time 3D scene built with Three.js and WebGL — glowing rings and a 3D dumbbell — which makes the brand feel premium before a single word is read.

Can the gym update the website without a developer?

Yes. The custom admin panel lets staff publish blog posts, upload photos and edit other site data themselves.

What does the chatbot do?

It answers common visitor questions — about memberships, timings and training — at any hour, so potential members get answers even when the front desk is closed.
